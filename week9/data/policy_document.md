# Home & Motor Combined Policy — Policy Wording (synthetic, for coursework)

## 1.1 Policy excess
Every settled claim is reduced by the policy excess shown on the policy schedule for that claim. The excess is deducted after any sub-limit cap is applied.

## 1.2 Sub-limits
Where a cover section states a sub-limit, the most we will pay for one event under that section is the sub-limit, before deduction of the excess. A claim whose assessed loss exceeds the sub-limit is settled as a partial payment.

## 3.1 Escape of water (internal)
We cover sudden and accidental damage caused by water escaping from internal sources such as burst pipes or failed appliances. Sub-limit GBP 8,100 per event. Excluded: damage that was pre-existing, intentional damage, and gradual seepage over time.

## 4.2a Storm
We cover damage caused by storm winds and storm-driven rain entering through damage caused by the storm itself.

## 4.2b Flood exclusion
We do not cover any damage caused by flood, meaning water entering the property from outside from rivers, creeks, the sea, surface run-off or overflowing drains. This applies whether the claim is declared as escape of water or otherwise. Flood cover requires the separate flood rider. Payout modifier 0.

## 5.1 Fire
We cover accidental fire damage. Excluded: electrical faults where required maintenance was not carried out, and properties left unoccupied for more than 90 consecutive days. Sub-limit GBP 100,000.

## 6.1 Accidental vehicle damage
We cover accidental damage to the insured vehicle. Excluded: racing, rallies and competitions, and commercial use without a commercial-use endorsement. Sub-limit GBP 50,000.

## 6.3 Intoxicated driving exclusion
We do not cover any loss or damage arising while the driver's blood-alcohol concentration exceeded the legal limit (0.08). This exclusion applies to all accident claims regardless of fault. Payout modifier 0.

## 7.1 Theft
We cover theft of insured property following forcible entry. Excluded: negligent security, and vehicle theft where keys were left inside an unattended vehicle. Sub-limit GBP 50,000.

## 7.4 Hail
We cover structural damage caused by hail. Purely cosmetic damage with no functional impact is excluded. Sub-limit GBP 30,000.

## 7.5 Vandalism
We cover malicious damage by third parties. Damage caused by the insured's own tenants is excluded. Sub-limit GBP 10,000.

## 7.6 Falling trees
We cover damage from storm-related tree fall. Excluded: trees the insured knew to be diseased or structurally unsound. Sub-limit GBP 15,000.

## 7.7 Lightning
We cover damage caused directly by a lightning strike. Electronics sub-limit GBP 2,300 per event. Surge damage where no whole-home surge suppressor was installed is excluded.

## 8.1 Arson and criminal acts exclusion
We do not cover fire or any other damage caused by arson or by any criminal act, whether committed by the insured or by a third party. Payout modifier 0. A claim under active criminal investigation is held, not denied, until the investigation outcome is confirmed.

## 9.1 Medical expenses
We cover reasonable medical expenses arising from an insured event. Conditions that pre-date the policy start date are excluded. Sub-limit GBP 25,000.
